</h2><p>In October 2024, the
<a class="inline-block" href="https://opensource.datadoghq.com/about/#the-community-open-source-engineering-team" rel="noopener" target="_blank">COSE (Community Open Source Engineering)</a>
team was formed with the mission to strengthen Vector&rsquo;s open source foundation and improve the developer experience.
Today, we&rsquo;re celebrating our first year by highlighting the contributions we&rsquo;ve made to the Vector community. The
COSE team has committed over <strong>550 changes</strong> to Vector, across <strong>8 major releases</strong> (0.43.0 through 0.51.0).</p>
